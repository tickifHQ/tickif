## Meet Tickif

**Tickif helps people discover real architecture, construction, and interior projects and connect with the professionals behind them.**

Homeowners can explore ideas, save favourites, and send enquiries. Professionals can showcase their portfolios, highlight completed work, and connect with potential clients.

**Real projects. The professionals behind them.**

## Our story

### A beautiful space is only part of the story.

A photograph can inspire you to imagine a home, a workspace or a fresh start. But when it’s time to create your own, you need more than inspiration. You want to understand what was built, what the project involved and who made it happen.

**That is the idea behind Tickif.**

We’re building a discovery and portfolio platform that brings together **architecture, construction and interiors**—connecting real project references with useful details and the professionals responsible for the work.

For people planning a space, our purpose is to make the next step clearer. Whether you’re collecting ideas, preparing to build or looking for a professional, we want to help you explore relevant work, understand its scope and start a better-informed conversation.

For professionals, we believe completed work deserves more than a collection of photographs. It deserves a portfolio that explains the project, shows their contribution and gives them proper credit. Architects, designers, construction companies and specialists should be recognised for the work they actually do.

**We’re starting with India and building with the people who shape its spaces.** Our focus is on useful project information, clear attribution and a community where homeowners and professionals can understand each other better.

Tickif is not here to replace the professional. We’re here to help people discover their work—and help that work tell its story.

**Because the next space that inspires someone could be yours.**
