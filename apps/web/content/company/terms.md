## Professionals and enquiries

Designers are independent professionals. Tickif does not design, construct, supervise or inspect your home. Sending an enquiry does not book a consultation or create a construction contract. Agree scope, fees, taxes, milestones, cancellation terms and warranties directly with the professional before starting work or paying them.

Check references, qualifications, required licences, permissions and written estimates. A profile badge, portfolio, rating or paid plan does not guarantee workmanship, safety, availability or regulatory approval. Professionals remain responsible for their claims and their obligations to clients.

Enquiries share the information you choose to provide with the selected professional and authorised workspace members. They may contact you about that enquiry. Do not submit another person’s contact details without permission or use enquiries for bulk marketing. A response, quote or successful engagement is not guaranteed.

## Community standards

Use Tickif honestly and respect people’s work, safety and privacy. You must not:

- Publish stolen portfolios, fabricated qualifications, fake reviews, misleading before-and-after images or undisclosed paid endorsements.
- Upload unlawful, threatening, discriminatory, abusive or sexually exploitative material, or expose private addresses, identity documents or contact details without a valid reason and appropriate permission.
- Impersonate a person or business, solicit fraudulent payments, send spam, distribute malware or evade restrictions through another account.
- Break access controls, collect private data, overload the service or scrape and reuse material in breach of rights or law.
- Present an AI-generated concept, render or materially altered image as a photograph of a completed real project. Describe conceptual and synthetic work clearly and preserve required labels and provenance.

Genuine criticism or disagreement is not, by itself, grounds to remove a review. Reviews should describe your experience without unsupported accusations or unrelated private information.
